# Warping Ocular
![icon.png](src/main/resources/assets/warping-ocular/icon.png) 
> Have you ever thought about how practically useless the spyglass is in modern Minecraft? This mod aims to fix that and turn it into a truly functional item.

**The mod introduces an item that inherits the behavior of the vanilla spyglass while featuring unique textures, models, and mechanics, as well as upgrade paths.**

**Warping Ocular:**

* The entire mod revolves around it 🥶🤓
* **Crafting:** Crafted with a spyglass in the center, an Eye of Ender in the top-right corner, 1 iron ingot above and to the right of the spyglass, and redstone dust in the bottom-left corner.
* **Appearance:** Resembles an iron scope with an Eye of Ender layered over the lens.
* **Usage:** Hold RMB while holding the item to look through it.
* **Mechanism:** Looking through the sight zooms in your vision and lets you view the world through the eyes of an Enderman.
* **Teleportation:** Aim at your desired location and left-click (LKM) to teleport.
* **Requirements:** Requires Ender Pearls in your inventory to activate.
* **Cooldown:** Goes on a 5-second cooldown after each teleportation.
* **Visuals:** Spawns particles around you upon teleporting.
* **Durability:** Consumes durability per use and starts with 64 max durability.
* **Enchantments:** Can be enchanted with Unbreaking, Mending, and Curse of Vanishing.
* **Repairing:** Can be repaired on an anvil using Amethyst Shards.
* **Combining:** Can be combined with another ocular on an anvil (vanilla mechanic).

**Farsightedness I & II:**

* Additional enchantments for the ocular that increase the maximum teleportation distance from the base 64 blocks up to 96 and 128 blocks, respectively.
* **Level I Book:** Obtained via a lore-friendly recipe (by applying the "Eye" armor trim using Lapis Lazuli to a book).
* **Level II Upgrade:** Created by combining two Level I books on an anvil or by applying a second Level I book directly to the ocular.

The mod runs on Fabric starting from Java 26.2 and requires Fabric API.
